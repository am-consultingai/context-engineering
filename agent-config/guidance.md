
## How to work in this workspace (read before starting any task)

- **Start from the maps.** Before exploring a repo, read its `arch/<repo>.md`. Treat the
  maps as your survey of each repo: do not re-survey structure a map already describes, and
  do not launch broad exploration subagents to map a repo. Open source files only to confirm
  the specific facts your answer relies on (paths, line numbers, signatures, commands), or
  where the map is silent. If a map turns out to be wrong, say so in your answer.
- **Repo conventions are mandatory.** Before planning or changing anything in a repo, read
  that repo's agent docs: `saleor/AGENTS.md` and `arch/saleor-graphql-conventions.md`,
  `saleor-dashboard/AGENTS.md`, `storefront/AGENTS.md`. Apply every rule that is relevant,
  and say in your plan which rules apply and how you follow them.
