We sell through several sales channels and want product ratings to be
per channel instead of global:

- A merchandiser should be able to set a product's rating separately for
  each channel the product is available in.
- The customer storefront product page should show the rating for the
  channel the customer is browsing.
- Existing ratings must not be lost, and existing API consumers must keep
  working while they migrate.

Treat this as a real, end-to-end feature across the backend, the dashboard
and the storefront. Before changing any code, give me your plan.
