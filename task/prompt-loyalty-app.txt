We want a loyalty-points program, built as a new application that integrates
with our Saleor stack:

- Customers earn points when an order is fully paid (e.g. 1 point per 1 unit
  of order total; merchandisers can change the rate per channel).
- Logged-in customers see their points balance on their storefront account
  page, and can redeem points for store credit they can use at checkout.
- Merchandisers see a customer's points balance and history inside the
  dashboard, on the customer's page, and can adjust it manually.
- Points must never be double-awarded or lost, even if events are delivered
  more than once, out of order, or while the app is down.
- It must run in our local development stack alongside everything else.

Design the architecture (components, where code lives, data ownership, how it
talks to Saleor, security) and give me an implementation plan across all the
repos involved. Before changing any code, give me your plan.
